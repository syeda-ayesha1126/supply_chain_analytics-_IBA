## 📚 Course Overview

This repository documents the analytical work completed throughout the **Supply Chain Analytics** course at **IBA Karachi**.

The work focuses on applying analytical methods to the supply chain of **Chilli Milli**, a product of **Ismail Industries Limited**, covering forecasting, inventory, sourcing networks, risk, ESG, and the final capstone project.
